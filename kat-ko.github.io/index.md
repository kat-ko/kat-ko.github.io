I am a PhD fellow in [Sebastian Risi's Lab](https://real.itu.dk) at the IT University of Copenhagen, working at the intersection of neuroscience and machine learning. My research focuses on <span class="spell">continual learning</span>, which is the ability of a network, whether biological or artificial, to continue learning without overwriting existing (important) knowledge.

My current work is guided by two key questions. The first concerns how <span class="spell">forgetting</span> and <span class="spell">transfer</span> manifest in the <span class="spell">representational geometry</span> of a network, specifically the dimensionality and shape of its task representations, as tasks are introduced sequentially. Alongside this, I am working on the foundations of the field. I ask what a task is, how cutting a learning stream into tasks determines what we end up measuring as forgetting and transfer, and how similarity between tasks can be measured in the first place.

Outside of research, I create art, some of which can be found [here](/art/).
